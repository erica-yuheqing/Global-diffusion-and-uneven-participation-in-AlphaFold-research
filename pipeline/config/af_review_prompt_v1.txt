DECISION LEVELS

Relevance:

- R1: Background/citation only. AlphaFold appears only as a landmark citation, general background example, incidental mention, or broad impact statement.

- R3: Actual downstream use. The study retrieves AlphaFold Database structures, runs AlphaFold/AlphaFold2/AlphaFold3/ColabFold, or integrates predictions into an empirical workflow.
- R4: Deep technical engagement. The study benchmarks, modifies, extends, stress-tests, or develops a method/tool around AlphaFold. Original AlphaFold model/database/platform papers also fall at R4 but must be marked original_platform and are not downstream adoption.

Evidence sufficiency:
- E0: No usable abstract/full-text evidence. A title alone does not establish actual use unless it unambiguously identifies the work as an AlphaFold-focused study; use uncertain when the required distinction cannot be made.
- E1: Weak or indirect evidence, such as an incidental title/metadata signal, general statement, future/hypothetical language, or unlinked citation.
- E2: Explicit evidence in a title or abstract that supports the decision, but procedural/full-text confirmation is absent or the exact use mode remains partly ambiguous.
- E3: Explicit, attributable evidence in supplied full-text blocks, methods/results language, or directly linked references that supports the decision and use mode.

PRIMARY LABELS

- FP: unrelated or abbreviation false positive.
- background_only: incidental/background mention or citation without substantive discussion or use.
- review_discussion: substantive discussion, review, commentary, news, perspective, or comparison without actual use by the study.
- database_consumption: the study actually obtains and analyzes precomputed AlphaFold/AlphaFold Database structures.

- original_platform: an original AlphaFold model, database, or official platform-development work; related but not downstream adoption.
- uncertain: available evidence cannot support a reliable distinction.

ADOPTION RULE

Adoption requires evidence of actual use by the study. At least one of the following must be supported:
1. obtaining and analyzing specific precomputed AlphaFold structures;
2. running AlphaFold, AlphaFold2, AlphaFold3, AlphaFold-Multimer, or ColabFold;
3. integrating predictions into docking, screening, experimental interpretation, or another empirical workflow;
4. benchmarking, modifying, extending, or developing a downstream method/tool around AlphaFold.

The following do not establish adoption by themselves:
- a landmark-paper citation;
- a general statement about AlphaFold's impact;
- "could", "may", "might", or other future/hypothetical use;
- review/editorial/news/perspective discussion without use by the study;
- appearance only in a bibliography;
- original AlphaFold/platform development.
