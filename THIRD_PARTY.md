# Sources, attribution and redistribution

| Source | Material used in the study | How to obtain or attribute it |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAlex | Work, institution, authorship, source and citation metadata. The deposit retains processed bibliographic IDs, some names and identifiers, links, observation dates, pagination/quality information and historical classifications. | Cite OpenAlex and record the relevant retrieval period. [OpenAlex data](https://openalex.org/data) · [OpenAlex documentation](https://developers.openalex.org/) |
| Publication evidence | Evidence available to the original coders from abstracts and accessible articles. | Reacquire through identifiers and lawful provider access. Original OpenAlex JSONL, publisher/PMC text, evidence passages and private annotation workbooks are excluded. Rights in each publication remain with its rights holders. |
| SCImago Journal & Country Rank | Historical journal position used in the article is an author calculation from full annual SJR lists, exact ISSN matching and category-specific midranks. | Attribute SCImago Journal & Country Rank. Download complete 2015–2025 annual exports, all countries, subjects and sources. Original CSVs and the two provider-wide ranking derivatives are supplied through reconstruction instructions. [SCImago rankings](https://www.scimagojr.com/journalrank.php) |
| World Bank | FY2020 income classification (2018 GNI). Authors' crosswalk preserves unresolved classifications and separate institutional output groups. | Cite the historical classification workbook and fiscal year. [World Bank country and lending groups](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519-world-bank-country-and-lending-groups) |
| Natural Earth | Map outlines used for geographic display. | Made with Natural Earth, public-domain map data. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) |

The MIT grant covers author-created software. The CC BY 4.0 grant covers author-created data. External packages installed by the environment retain their own licenses.
